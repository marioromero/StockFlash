## Approved source / rationale
Part 1 §7.4 RN-26 and §8.3 INV-06 (P0): sending deducts physical stock at origin, receiving adds stock at destination, quantities not received remain as outstanding difference. Approved lifecycle `draft → requested → in_transit → received | partially_received | cancelled`. Part 2 §7.5 `stock_transfers` and `stock_transfer_items` field/type/constraint specification. Reuse CORESEQ-001 `DocumentSequenceAllocator` for `transfer` scoped to origin branch, StockLedger for immutable `transfer_out`/ `transfer_in` physical movements, InventoryWriteGuard, existing RBAC/branch/module checks. INVCORE/INVWRITE/INVKARDEX/INVREORDER/INVCSV already VERIFIED_TECH. This packet is **one usable transfer flow**; avoid unnecessary scaffolding and unrelated modules.

## EARS
- WHEN a valid requested transfer is dispatched, the system MUST atomically record negative transfer_out movements at the origin only once and set in_transit.
- WHEN a valid in_transit transfer is received, the system MUST atomically record positive transfer_in movements at the destination only for actual received quantities, preserving pending differences.
- WHEN receiving less than dispatched, the transfer MUST reflect partially_received and preserve discrepancy, not invent stock or auto-resolve.
- WHEN a transition fails access/branch/status/validation/license/audit/duplicate checks, no partial status or physical stock changes may persist.
- WHEN a user only has access to the destination branch, they may receive authorized incoming inventory but may not dispatch from an unauthorized origin.
