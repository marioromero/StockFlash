## EARS acceptance
- WHEN a permitted user creates or edits a valid person/company, the customer and its group/discount settings MUST persist transactionally, with RUT encrypted and blind indexed when provided.
- WHEN two differently formatted equivalent RUTs are registered, duplicates MUST be rejected without exposing the stored RUT.
- WHEN a user lacking `customers.view_sensitive` views customers, RUT and contact MUST remain masked and no cleartext PII delivered in response payload; each authorized reveal MUST be audited.
- WHEN an invalid discount, unknown group, unauthorized actor, inactive license/module or write/audit failure occurs, no partial customer/group change MUST persist.
- WHEN no sale schema exists, customer UI MUST NOT fabricate purchase history or stock reservations.
