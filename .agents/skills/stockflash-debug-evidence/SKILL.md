# stockflash-debug-evidence

Isolate failing behavior; capture expected vs actual, relevant diff/paths, at most three hypotheses and the smallest decisive test. Prefer targeted file reads and bounded logs, never secrets or full transcripts. Escalate material schema/spec/fiscal/security contradictions. After resolution, store one brief sanitized learning linked to feature/SHA when useful, not every thought or repeated investigation.
